## What the papers do and do not establish

- **ResNet + Attention** — P4 (SLCAM-AquaNet) states ResNet18/50 backbones and a four-branch SLCAM (horizontal, vertical, spatial 7×7 conv, channel avg+max MLP) with spatial and channel paths fused; the exact fusion formula and pretraining are NOT STATED in the extracted text. Our composition (channel gate, then location×spatial gates) is marked as ours.
- **YOLO** — no supplied paper uses YOLO; P5 mentions it only as the supervised alternative it avoids. No dataset here has bounding boxes. YOLO therefore acts as a feature-extraction backbone only (CSPDarknet C2f/SPPF blocks re-implemented in-repo). No `ultralytics` dependency was added, no detection head exists, no boxes are used or fabricated, and no pretrained YOLO weights are loaded (none are available offline without that package).
- **ViT / Transformer** — P5 uses a frozen DINOv2 ViT-S/14 for unsupervised anomaly detection (k-NN over patch features); it is not a supervised classifier and is not combined with a CNN or LSTM. Our Transformer encoders are ViT-style blocks trained from scratch over CNN/YOLO feature-map tokens.
- **LSTM / BiLSTM** — no supplied paper uses one; single images carry no temporal sequence. The sequence modelled here is the 7×7 feature map read in raster order — an adaptation, stated as such.

Dependencies added: **none** (torch + torchvision only). Interface to the experiment runner: `.classifier` and `backbone_parameters()` mirror the staged-fine-tuning contract; wiring the runner (which is typed to torchvision `EfficientNet`) to these models is the next phase's work.

torch 2.14.0; smoke-tested on CPU and Apple MPS.
